## Hi, I'm Kasia

I'm a 2nd year PhD student in cancer genomics and computational biology.

My work focuses on computational analyses of genomic and epigenetic data. Currently I work primarily with whole-exome sequencing, microarray and long-read Oxford Nanopore data to determine the alterations in histologically normal tissues adjacent to breast and bladder tumors. The goal of my current research is to assess the extent of genomic and epigenetic alterations in the cancerized field with the intention to find potential early-detection strategies or therapy targets.
